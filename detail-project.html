01 / Overview

One product, several daily tasks.

A point-of-sale tool sits at the center of day-to-day retail work. Beyond processing a sale, store teams may need to check products, monitor stock, review performance, and keep operations organized.

For Aleighty, the design direction explores how a mobile-first POS experience can bring these common tasks into a more approachable interface.

02 / The challenge

Make essential actions easy to find.

Retail workflows can become harder to manage when sales, products, stock, and reports feel disconnected. The design challenge is to help users move between frequent tasks without making the interface feel heavy.

01 / CLARITY

Keep tasks clear

Make core actions recognizable and straightforward to navigate.

02 / INFORMATION

Surface useful data

Give sales and inventory information a clear place in the experience.

03 / USABILITY

Support mobile use

Keep layouts readable and practical on smaller screens.

04 / FLEXIBILITY

Leave room to grow

Consider how additional operational tools could fit into the product.

03 / Design direction

A focused interface for frequent workflows.

The proposed direction uses a simple hierarchy, compact information blocks, and recognizable navigation patterns. The goal is to keep the interface calm while giving high-frequency actions enough visibility.
