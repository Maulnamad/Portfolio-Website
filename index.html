Designing with purpose, building with curiosity.

I'm Maul, a Product Designer with a background in UI/UX design. I enjoy turning business requirements and user needs into clear, thoughtful digital experiences.

Over the years, I've worked across different product types, from mobile apps to business tools. My process connects the dots between understanding a problem, mapping a flow, shaping an interface, and making ideas tangible through prototypes.
